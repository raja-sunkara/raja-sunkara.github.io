I am a Ph.D. candidate in **Computer Science** at Missouri S&T, advised by [Prof. Ardhendu Tripathy](https://astripathy.github.io) in the [Statistical Machine Learning Lab](https://sites.mst.edu/smilelab/). I work on **sequential decision making**: algorithms for **black-box optimization** and **multi-armed bandits** with provable guarantees, and applying them to real problems in machine learning, including **large language models**.

My recent work, [Adaptive Partitioning Schemes for Optimistic Optimization](https://raja-sunkara.github.io/Learned-Partitions-SequOOL/) (ICML 2025), learns the low-dimensional structure of a black-box function and uses it to search faster, with better regret bounds than SequOOL. The code is a pip-installable package ([GitHub](https://github.com/raja-sunkara/Learned-Partitions-SequOOL)).

Before my Ph.D., I completed a thesis M.S. at Missouri S&T with [Prof. Tie Luo](https://tluocs.github.io) on computer vision under adverse conditions. That work produced [SPD-Conv](https://github.com/LabSAINT/SPD-Conv) (ECML PKDD 2022), a CNN building block for small objects and low-resolution images that is widely used in YOLO-based detectors, and [YOGA](https://github.com/TrustAIoT/YOGA_object-detection) (Pattern Recognition 2023), a lightweight object detector for edge devices.

I earned my B.Tech. in **Aerospace Engineering** from the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, with a thesis supervised by [Prof. Ranjith Mohan](https://home.iitm.ac.in/ranjith.m/md/ranj.html). Between degrees I spent about four years in industry, as a data analyst, data scientist and research engineer at Enfrien Innovations, Agrometrics and Matdun Labs, and as an R&D data science intern at Alcon.

**Research interests:** black-box and Bayesian optimization · multi-armed bandits and pure exploration · reinforcement learning · efficient deep learning for vision and LLMs.
